About

The VIBE delivers high-energy Top 40 hits from the '70s to today, covering artists like Stevie Wonder, Bruno Mars, Teddy Swims, and Dua Lipa. Electric energy, packed dance floors, and unforgettable nights.

Alex "The Shredder"

Guitar / Sound

Born and raised in the local Portuguese community, Alex found music at just six years old. A multi-instrumentalist on guitar, bass, drums, and piano, he was performing alongside renowned Portuguese artists at some of the area's largest festivals by age 13, and has played and recorded with several respected Portuguese artists. He has also performed with groups including Jen and Tonic. Off stage you'll find him at the gym, walking his dogs, or out on his motorcycle.

Joe

Drums

Inspired by his father, Joe started playing drums at a young age and studied at Ragosta Music. His background spans concert, marching, and jazz bands, plus years in original and cover bands. He has recorded studio albums and played festivals, clubs, casinos, and venues across multiple states, and spent over a decade performing Americana music. Now he's back on the cover band scene with The VIBE.

Peter

Bass

Every great band needs someone holding it all together, and for The VIBE, that's Peter. A Rhode Island native, his love of music started in Bristol, where Grand Funk Railroad inspired him to form a garage band with neighborhood friends. That grew into a lifelong passion and stages across New England. His steady groove and upbeat personality bring every song to life, whether it's a classic rock anthem, a soulful ballad, or today's biggest hits.

Tim

Keys / LinnStrument

With a background in jazz and a love for pushing musical boundaries, Tim blends traditional keys with modern synths, custom sounds, and his signature LinnStrument. He can go from classic piano and organ to full-blown modern production without missing a beat, recreating parts that seemingly require three extra musicians with just ten fingers. If you're wondering what that crazy-looking instrument onstage is, that's probably Tim.
